## Verdict

The design correctly resolves the three spec-level contradictions (companion interfaces, `ListByState` rejection, separate last-active table) — all six reviewers independently confirmed those. But the design does **not** resolve the review findings: 19 findings are unaddressed or contradicted by design text, including the Critical lease schema that fails the spec's own acceptance test on SQL (row 1), four independently-confirmed High defects (rows 2, 3, 5, 7), the pre-MFA/lockout anchor coupling (row 4), and the compliance Highs (rows 8, 9). The design shipped the lease flaw verbatim, claims false transitive ceremony coverage, and retains the "never wrong-deprovisioning" overclaim the reviewers disproved. No review-response loop exists (reviews postdate the design). Implementation must not proceed; the design must be amended to fix the lease schema, cursor ordering/state filter, WebAuthn third anchor (or explicit scope-out with rationale), Touch placement, telemetry/readiness surfaces, freshness guard, and PURGED/erasure handling, with the conformance/race tests the reviewers specified.

VERDICT: FAIL - Critical lease schema cannot exclude concurrent holders (QA F1); cursor no-op-prefix starvation breaks O(k) (QA F2/perf F-P1); standalone WebAuthn ceremony bypasses both Touch anchors (QA F6/DB F-DB-1/security S2/SRE F1); pre-MFA anchor resets dormancy+lockout on credential presentation (S1); fail-open Touch with frozen signal enables silent mass wrong-deprovisioning with no freshness guard or alarm (SRE F3); zero lifecycle telemetry and silent lost lease (SRE F2/QA F5); durable store lacks /readyz, storage-health, and CheckSchema/MaxVersion gates (SRE F4); durable PURGED rows falsify the erasure claim (C-P1); unbounded synchronous login write with no latency bound (F-P2); plus unresolved Mediums: index contradiction (F3), re-created-id ghost (F-DS-3), clock-skew overclaim (F-DS-2), no reactivation (F-DB-2), datamap/notice gaps (C-P3/C-P5). None of the High/Critical findings are resolved or dismissed with reasons in the design.
