## Blocking issues

1. **B1 (High, all reviewers): the purge does not erase the primary credential, and the delete rule removes the only marker at the worst moment.** The eraser has no password-credential leg; after purge+delete, old credentials re-authenticate, the user record is re-created, the account reads ACTIVE with no tombstone and no MFA (erased). The design's headline invariant — "PURGED means erased, enforced when armed" — is false for the primary credential. Fix is precedented in-tree (`PasswordCredentialDeleter`, used by `internal/adminuser/service.go`); the delete rule must require credential deletion.
2. **B2 (High): the crash/restart story is factually wrong.** The designed crash window cannot persist an orphan (in-memory store); the real window (reaction-mode Append→crash→no erase) is unlisted and leaves an un-erased subject reading ACTIVE with no audit; restart silently converts INVITED→ACTIVE once direction 1 gates it. "Inherited" dismissal does not cover direction 2 making these states production-reachable.
3. **B3 (High): the Decision 3.3 repair guarantee is unreachable in stock wiring** (admin 404, SCIM new-id, non-PURGED conflict refused), so orphaned records accumulate permanently and the fail-closed residual ("repaired on next provisioning") is vacuous — with no admin-gated reaper or fail-closed `Delete` treatment specified.

Plus two required release conditions: the direction-1 dependency (B1's gate interplay, interim INVITED denial, ordering test) and the purge-restore fencing/topology declaration (DS F-DS-1); and a mandatory pre-implementation amendment: a legal home for `NewInviteSeeder` (architect F1) or the first commit fails the architecture gate.

VERDICT: FAIL - B1 password credential survives purge (delete rule 3.2 removes the tombstone exactly when re-login resurrects the account as ACTIVE; "PURGED means erased" false for the primary credential), B2 crash/restart analysis factually wrong (real reaction-mode crash window unlisted: un-erased subject reads ACTIVE with no audit; restart converts INVITED→ACTIVE), B3 tombstone repair unreachable in stock wiring (admin 404s, SCIM mints new ids, non-PURGED conflict refused — orphans accumulate; "repaired on next provisioning" vacuous), plus required conditions: direction-1 same-release/interim-INVITED-denial, purge-restore fencing or unsupported-topology declaration, and the serverbuildplatform file-home amendment (architect F1) before any commit.
